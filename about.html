ABOUT BLOGAUTH

Engineering deserves thoughtful writing.

BlogAuth exists to publish software architecture analyses, post-mortems, and engineering journals that prioritize correctness, depth, long-term relevance, and clarity rather than trends or transient noise.

We publish knowledge that remains valuable years from now.

Long-form thinking is at the core of our publishing philosophy. Modern software architectures are layered with complexity that cannot be compressed into quick summaries or listicles. We dedicate time to breaking down systems step-by-step.

Technical depth is our baseline. Our writers are active practitioners who operate, benchmark, and break systems in production. We speak directly to engineers who look for raw configuration files, compiler states, and empirical metrics.

Engineering culture thrives on transparent knowledge sharing. By detailing distributed consensus failures, lock contention issues, and database tuning choices, we build a technical library that helps teams ship more reliable software.

Directives

Editorial Principles

Accuracy

Every article prioritizes correctness over publication speed. We compile and verify code snippets in staging runtimes before sharing them.

Depth

We explain systems internals rather than surface-level tutorials. Our goal is to detail why a tool works, how it breaks, and its exact tradeoffs.

Clarity

Complex abstractions should become understandable. We design readable structures, clean layout geometry, and precise editorial visual systems.

Longevity

We build resources that remain useful years down the line. We focus on architectural patterns, computer science basics, and timeless concepts.

Rigor

Writing Standards

Peer reviewed

Articles undergo editorial review by senior system architects prior to distribution.

Technically verified

Benchmarks and terminal traces are validated under monitored hardware runs.

Production experience

Manifestos are derived from live systems experiences rather than theoretical setups.

Original diagrams

Network topologies and CPU pipelines are illustrated with custom-drawn visual assets.

Performance focused

We evaluate time complexity, lock waits, memory footprint profiles, and overheads.

Open references

All references cite verified RFC standards, kernel documentations, and code commits.
